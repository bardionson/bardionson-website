## Balloons In The Sky — NFC Summit Lisbon

**May 27–29, 2026 · Unicorn Factory Lisbon, Lisbon, Portugal**

Presented by HASH Gallery at NFC Summit 2026, **Balloons In The Sky** is the third and final act of The Simulation Trilogy, following *Painting With Fire* and *Bones In The Sky*.

A custom StyleGAN model trained on 3,700 photographs from the Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta generates a continuous stream of balloon imagery on a 75" screen. Visitors press a button to freeze the stream, instantly minting a 1/1 NFT on Ethereum mainnet and receiving an archival dye-sublimation print — a physical bridge to a digital moment.

Onsite mints sold out over the course of the event, with 171 NFTs minted live by attendees.
