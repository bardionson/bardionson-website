**Justin Aversano and Bård Ionson**

*Future*, a collaboration between Justin Aversano and Bård Ionson, is on view in **OCCUPY 2** at **NODE, Palo Alto, California**.

### On view

- November 27–29, 2026
- December 18–20, 2026

NODE’s second OCCUPY presentation brings together nine artists selected from its fall open call, showing work across three exhibition spaces in Palo Alto over two weekends.
