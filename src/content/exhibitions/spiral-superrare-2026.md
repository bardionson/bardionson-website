## ./spiral

**SuperRare · Fondazione Aversano, New York · September 2026**

Curated by Atay Ilgun and Jonathan Carmel (jonooo)

Eleven artists return to formative works and processes, treating the past as material for what comes next: Anne Spalter, Gene Kogan, Pindar Van Arman, Artonymous, Helena Sarin, Rhea Myers, Atay Ilgun, Kyle McDonald, Robbie Barrat, Mario Klingemann, and **Bård Ionson**.

./spiral opens at Fondazione Aversano, New York, on Sept 3, 2026 and runs through Sept 10, 2026; the full online release follows via [SuperRare](https://superrare.com) on Sept 11, 2026.

Responding to the spiral brief, the artists revisit formative tools, methods and unresolved ideas through new digital and physical iterations, including interactive systems, ceramics and print-based works.
