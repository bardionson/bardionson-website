**Bård and Jennifer Ionson** have been named among the **63 semi-finalists in the inaugural .ART Award**, with *Balloons In The Sky*.

The .ART Registry's November 19 announcement reports that the jury selected the semi-finalists from close to 2,000 applications. The award considers the artwork, its creative process, and the artist's practice through a website on a .ART domain.

Our entry is *Balloons In The Sky*, an interactive generative installation created together. Visitors choose a moment from its continuously changing stream of images, capturing a work as a physical print and an NFT.
