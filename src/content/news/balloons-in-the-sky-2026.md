# Balloons In The Sky

**Balloons In The Sky** is the third and final part of The Simulation Trilogy, following *Painting With Fire* and *Bones In The Sky*. It's a self-contained, autonomous art-delivery system — a custom StyleGAN architecture navigates a multi-dimensional map of original digital and analog source images, and each capture simultaneously mints a 1/1 NFT on Ethereum mainnet and prints a physical artifact via an integrated dye-sublimation printer.
