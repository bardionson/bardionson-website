## Kill Them All

Inspired by these and more Lawrence Lee created "Kill Them All". Lawrence used ProCreate on an iPad and Photoshop to draw a new version of Putin. Added the Kremlin, the Hammer and Sickle, rockets firing and the words. "Kill Them All". The core version will be sold as prints at NFT Paris.

## An Exhibition

About the time we were getting close to finished piece [Eleonora Brizi](https://www.breezyart.io/) was looking for art to place into the new SuperRare Space "[Criticize by Creating](https://superrare.com/spaces/0x23ff63c7089d5643c12e863707e116c109b29a0a/)" that used written words. This exhibition will appear at NFT Paris 2023. In this Space and Exhibition there would need to be three variations. One will be sold at NFT Paris, one will be in the permanent collection of "Criticize by Creating" and the third will be owned by the artists, Bård Ionson and Lawrence Lee.

To create a video version to sell I created a video of the best faces of Putin morphing into each other as he becomes more grotesque as his evil grows. But it was not quite right.
